## What it costs in Union City, in ranges

It is worth walking into this conversation with a range in your head. That way,
when someone gives you a price, you know whether you are in reasonable
territory.

Per ranges published by U.S. News, Bankrate, NerdWallet and Insurify, average
annual NJ premiums run roughly $1,400–$1,600 for minimum coverage and roughly
$3,100–$3,800 for full coverage. Those are statewide averages, not a quote for
you.

That range is wide on purpose, because price moves with concrete factors:

- **The exact ZIP code.** Two ZIPs in the same city do not price the same.
- **Annual mileage and use.** Driving to job sites daily is not the same as driving to the store, and reporting it wrong can cost you a claim.
- **How you pay.** Paying six months in full almost always comes out differently than monthly with an installment fee.
- **Driving history.** Tickets, crashes and prior claims matter, though less than people expect next to the ZIP code.
- **Who else drives the car.** Household drivers listed on the policy change the math.

Those factors explain why two neighbors pay differently. What does not change
between neighbors is the legal minimum.
