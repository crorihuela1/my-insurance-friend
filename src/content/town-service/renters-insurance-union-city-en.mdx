## What it costs in Union City, in ranges

It is worth walking into this conversation with a range in your head. That way,

Renters policies in NJ and NY are commonly published in ranges of roughly $12 to

That range is wide on purpose, because price moves with concrete factors:

- **The contents limit.** Covering $15,000 of belongings does not cost the same as covering $50,000.
- **The deductible.** A higher deductible lowers the monthly payment and raises what comes out of your pocket at claim time.
- **The building.** Age, construction type and whether it has alarms or sprinklers move the number.
- **Whether you have a pet.** Some breeds affect the liability side of the policy.
- **Whether you work from home.** Business equipment in the home can fall outside a standard renters policy.

Price matters less than the fine print: what decides whether it helps you is the
scope of coverage.

## What it covers and what it does not

Unlike auto insurance, there is no legal minimum here. What governs is the lease
and what the policy says.

A standard renters policy splits into three parts: **contents** (your furniture,
clothes, electronics and personal tools), **personal liability** (if damage
starts in your unit and affects someone else) and **additional living expenses**
(if the place becomes uninhabitable).

What it leaves out matters just as much: **flood is not covered** under a
standard policy, neither is earthquake damage, and usually neither is equipment
for a business you run from home. The building owner's policy covers the
structure, not your belongings.

Flood is the exclusion that surprises people most. It is bought separately, and
in low-lying areas that difference decides whether you replace your furniture or
not.
