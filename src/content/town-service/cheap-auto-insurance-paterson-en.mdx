## Why insurance costs more in Paterson

Route 19, I-80 and Broadway start filling at 5:30 in the morning. People driving
to job sites in Bergen, to warehouses in Secaucus, to shifts that start before
sunrise. In this city the car is the tool that lets you work, which is why the
question is rarely *whether* to insure it but how to pay for it.

Carriers do not price Paterson based on what you do behind the wheel. They price
the ZIP code. The 07501 through 07505 ZIPs tend to come in above the state average
for three reasons that have nothing to do with your record: traffic density, claim
frequency in the area, and local repair costs. A driver with a perfect record in
Paterson can pay more than a driver with an at-fault crash in a Morris County
suburb.

There is a second factor that weighs heavily here and that almost nobody talks
about: **coverage lapses.** When someone cancels the policy for three months
because money got tight, then re-insures, New Jersey treats that gap as a risk
factor. The price on return is higher, and it stays higher for several years. It
is one of the most expensive things that happens in 07501, and it almost always
started as one month of savings.

And a third: a lot of people here end up paying **month to month**, with an
installment fee charged on every payment that never shows up in the price
comparison when you shop.

Read that second point again, because it hits everyone renewing in Paterson after
**January 1, 2026**: liability minimums rise from 25/50/25 to **35/70/25**. That
is not optional and it is not a carrier upsell, it is the state's new legal floor.
Plenty of people will see it as an unexplained increase at renewal when it is
actually the minimum changing.

PIP is worth pausing on. New Jersey is a **no-fault** state: your medical costs
after a crash are paid by your own PIP, regardless of who caused it. PIP is
mandatory and it is one reason NJ insurance costs more than neighboring states.
It is also the part of the policy where options vary most, and where a licensed
agent can explain what each level means.

That is the statewide average, not a quote. In Paterson, the real numbers we see
tend to land above those averages. What moves your particular number:

- **The exact ZIP code.** 07501 and 07514 do not price the same.
- **Annual mileage and use.** Driving 25,000 miles a year to job sites prices
  differently than 6,000 city miles. Reporting it wrong is a quiet way to create
  problems with a claim.
- **Coverage lapses,** as described above.
- **The vehicle model.** Not what the car cost, but what it costs to repair and
  how often that model gets stolen.
- **How you pay.** Paying six months in full almost always comes out differently
  than paying monthly with an installment fee.

## Foreign license, ITIN and consular ID

This is the question we get most from Paterson, so here it is directly: **some New
Jersey carriers will issue a policy using a foreign license, ITIN or consular ID,
and others will not.** There is no single statewide answer; it depends on which
companies the agent working with you can place business with.

New Jersey has also issued driver's licenses regardless of immigration status
since 2021, alongside the standard and Real ID license. Which document you hold
changes which companies can quote you, and that is exactly what a licensed agent
can check with you before you pay anything.
