## Why insurance costs what it costs in New Brunswick

New Brunswick traffic is college-town traffic: pedestrians, cyclists, narrow
streets and heavy movement at odd hours. Low-severity claim frequency here is
high, and carriers price that into 08901.

New Brunswick sits in Middlesex County, with roughly 55,266 residents and ZIP
codes 08901, 08903. About 53% of the population is Hispanic, which changes the
conversation: here the paperwork gets done in Spanish or it doesn't get done. A
lot of the people who contact us from New Brunswick also move between Perth
Amboy, Elizabeth and Newark, so what applies here rarely stays here.

In a city this compact the car lives on the street. Parallel parking, mirror
clips, catalytic converter theft and vandalism are the number one reason people
here ask about comprehensive rather than liability. And the density itself is
part of why the ZIP code prices the way it does.

## What it costs in New Brunswick, in ranges

Before the numbers, a useful warning: what follows are published statewide
ranges, not a quote. Your number depends on your situation.

Per ranges published by U.S. News, Bankrate, NerdWallet and Insurify, average
annual NJ premiums run roughly $1,400–$1,600 for minimum coverage and roughly
$3,100–$3,800 for full coverage. Those are statewide averages, not a quote for
you.

That range is wide on purpose, because price moves with concrete factors:

- **The exact ZIP code.** Two ZIPs in the same city do not price the same.
- **Annual mileage and use.** Driving to job sites daily is not the same as driving to the store, and reporting it wrong can cost you a claim.
- **Coverage lapses.** A gap without insurance raises the price and keeps it raised for years.
- **The vehicle model.** Not what it cost, but what it costs to repair and how often that model gets stolen.
- **How you pay.** Paying six months in full almost always comes out differently than monthly with an installment fee.

What is not negotiable is the floor. The state sets a minimum you have to carry
either way.
