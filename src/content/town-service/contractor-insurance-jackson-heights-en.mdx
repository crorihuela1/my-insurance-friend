## What it costs in Jackson Heights, in ranges

The numbers below are statewide reference ranges. They are useful for knowing
whether a price you were given is normal, not for predicting yours.

For a small NJ crew, contractor GL policies are commonly published in ranges of
roughly $900 to $3,000 per year, with workers' comp calculated separately on
payroll. The range depends on trade, payroll, years of experience and claims
history.

That range is wide on purpose, because price moves with concrete factors:

- **The trade.** Roofing, demolition and work at height price differently than painting or finish carpentry.
- **The limits you carry.** Going from $500,000 to $1,000,000 does not double the cost, but it moves the number.
- **Whether you use subcontractors.** A sub without their own policy usually ends up counted in your auditable payroll.
- **The property type you work on.** Residential, commercial and institutional carry different requirements and prices.
- **Whether you need endorsements.** Additional insured and waiver of subrogation are policy changes, not just paperwork.

What is not negotiable is the minimum. The state sets one floor and your client
sets another, usually higher.

On the client side, the working standard almost always sits above the state

Worth keeping a copy of the registration and the certificate on your phone. Most
