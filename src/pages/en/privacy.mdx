## Who we are

This site is operated by PLACEHOLDER Seguros LLC. **We are not a licensed
insurance agency or broker.** We connect people looking for insurance with
agents and agencies that are licensed in New Jersey and New York.

## What we collect

When you fill out the quote form or message us on WhatsApp, we collect only what
you give us:

- **Name and phone number.** So the agent can reach you.
- **City.** To connect you with an agent who works your area.
- **Business type or vehicle.** To route you to someone who handles that risk.
- **Whether you currently have insurance and whether you need a COI.** So the
  agent knows how urgent your situation is.
- **Best time to reach you and preferred language.**

We do not ask for your Social Security number, driver's license number, banking
details or medical information. **We also do not ask what coverage you want**,
because that conversation belongs to a licensed agent, not to us.

We also automatically record:

- **A hashed identifier derived from your IP address.** We store the processed
  value, not the address itself.
- **The page you submitted from** and campaign parameters (UTM) if you arrived
  from an ad.
- **The date, time, language and exact consent text** you agreed to.

## What we use it for

One thing: connecting you with a licensed insurance agent and following up on
that connection. We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers
or data brokers.

## Who we share it with

- **The licensed agent or agency** we refer you to. That is why you contacted
  us, and that transfer is the service.
- **Our customer tracking system (CRM)**, which stores the information for us.
- **Where the law requires it**, such as a valid court order.

Once the licensed agent contacts you, that relationship is governed by that
agency's privacy policy, not this one.

## Calls and text messages

By checking the consent box on the form, you authorize us and the licensed
agents we refer you to to contact you at the number you gave us, including calls
and texts sent using automated systems.

**That consent is not a condition of buying anything.** You can withdraw it at
any time by replying **STOP** to any text message, or by writing to us. Message
and data rates may apply from your carrier.

## How long we keep it

We keep your consent record (the text, date and time) for **five years**,
because it is the evidence that you gave us permission to contact you. Other
contact information is kept while it remains useful for follow-up, and deleted
when you ask.

## Your rights

You can ask us, at no cost, to:

- **See** what information we hold about you.
- **Correct** anything that is wrong.
- **Delete** your information.
- **Stop** calls and messages.

Write to PLACEHOLDER@example-placeholder.com and we respond within the time
frames the law requires. If you live in New Jersey, the New Jersey Data Privacy
Act may give you additional rights.

## Cookies and measurement

This site uses no advertising cookies and does not track you across other sites.
If we add analytics in the future, we will say so here before doing it.

## Minors

This site is not directed at anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect
data from minors.

## Changes

If we change this policy we update the date above. If a change affects how we
use data you already gave us, we will tell you.
