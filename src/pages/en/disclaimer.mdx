## We are not an insurance agency

PLACEHOLDER Seguros LLC is **not a licensed insurance agency or broker** in New
Jersey, New York, or any other state. We do not sell policies, issue them,
service them, or handle claims.

What we do is connect you with agents and agencies who **are** licensed to do all
of that.

## This is not insurance advice

Everything on this site is general, educational information. We explain:

- what the law requires in New Jersey and New York,
- what price ranges are publicly published,
- and how the process of getting a policy works.

What we do **not** do, because we are not licensed to:

- tell you what coverage to buy,
- tell you how much coverage you need,
- give you a quote or tell you what you will pay,
- evaluate your particular situation.

That belongs to a licensed agent, after reviewing your case.

## How we get paid

We receive a **flat referral or advertising fee** from the agencies we work with.

That fee **does not change** based on:

- whether you buy a policy,
- which policy you buy,
- what you pay for it.

We say it that plainly for a reason: when someone is paid a percentage of your
premium, they have an incentive for you to buy more. We do not have one.

**You pay us nothing.** Using this site and getting connected is free.

## Affiliate links

Some pages may include links to insurance companies that pay us a commission if
you buy through them. When that happens we say so on that same page, **above**
the first link, as the FTC requires. It does not change the price you pay.

## About the legal information we cite

We cite statutes, regulations and bulletins with their official source, and every
fact carries a review date. Laws change. If you are making a decision based on
something you read here, confirm it with the official source or with a licensed
agent.
