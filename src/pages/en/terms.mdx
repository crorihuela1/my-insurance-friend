## 1. What we provide

We connect people looking for insurance with licensed insurance agents and
agencies in New Jersey and New York. That is the whole service.

## 2. What we do not do

**We are not a licensed insurance agency or broker.** We do not sell policies,
issue them, service them, collect premiums, or handle claims. We also do not give
insurance advice: we do not tell you what coverage to buy or how much you need.
Only a licensed agent can do that after reviewing your situation.

Information on this site is **educational and general**. It describes what the
law requires, what price ranges are published, and how the process works. It is
not a quote, not an offer of coverage, and not a contract.

## 3. How we get paid

We receive a **flat referral or advertising fee** from the agencies we connect
you with. That fee:

- does not depend on whether you buy a policy,
- does not depend on which policy you buy,
- does not depend on what you pay,
- and is not charged to you. Using this site costs you nothing.

Before making a decision, confirm with a licensed agent or with the official
source we link to.
