## Why this site exists

If you search for "contractor insurance" in Spanish, what you find is almost
always one of two things: a page machine-translated from English, or a phone
number with no explanation. Nothing that tells you what the law requires, what it
normally costs, or what they'll ask for on the job site.

This site exists to fill that gap. Every page is written for someone who lives
and works here, with state law cited and linked to its official source.

## What we do exactly

We collect a few facts from you and connect you with a **licensed** insurance
agent in your state who handles your kind of case. That agent reviews your
situation, explains the options, and issues the policy if you decide to buy.

## What we don't do

We don't sell policies, issue them, or handle claims. We're not an agency or a
broker. And we don't tell you what to buy: that requires a license and we don't
have one.

## How we make money

Agencies pay us a **flat fee** per referral. It isn't commission: it doesn't rise
if you buy a more expensive policy and it doesn't disappear if you buy nothing.

We say so on every page for a practical reason. When someone is paid a percentage
of your premium, they have an interest in you buying more. We don't, and we'd
rather you knew that up front.

## Where this is going

Today we operate as a referral service. The plan is to obtain an agency license
in New Jersey and be able to handle your case end to end. When that happens we'll
say so here, because it changes what we can legally do for you.
